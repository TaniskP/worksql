Terms

WorkSQL is a paid practice product for SQL Server. These terms are short on purpose.

The product

You write T-SQL against a fictional company, Harbour & Co. The first 10 tickets are free. A paid plan unlocks the remaining tickets. Queries run in our sandbox, never against your data.

Accounts and access

There is no password sign-up. Paying through Stripe unlocks this browser. If you lose the cookie, restore access from the pricing page with the checkout email. Don’t buy a second time for the same email.

Billing

Prices are in GBP. Monthly is £9.99. Yearly is £79. You can cancel from Stripe’s billing page. Paid access lasts until the period you bought ends.

Use

The site, tickets and reference pages are for your own learning. Don’t scrape the catalogue to republish it. The sandbox is not a place to run other people’s production queries.
