Support

Two ways in: workplace practice in the inbox, or guided lessons one ticket at a time. Both use the same Harbour & Co tickets.

All systems normal.

Talk to a person

Email us and a real person replies, usually within a few hours on weekdays.

I paid, but this browser is locked

Clearing cookies signs you out. Use Already paid on the pricing page with the email from checkout. We’ll send a 15-minute restore link. Don’t buy again.

My progress disappeared

Progress is kept in the browser you practised in. If you saved it by email, open Progress on the new device, ask for a link with the same email, and everything comes back. Paid members get this automatically: restoring access also brings back progress.

Something isn’t working

If a query hangs or the sandbox says it restarted, press Execute again. It recovers on its own within a few seconds. If it keeps happening, email us with the ticket name and your stack (SQL Server, PostgreSQL or MySQL).

Where do I start?

Workplace practice opens the inbox. Guided lessons lets you pick a lesson and learn step by step. The first 10 tickets are free. No sign-up.

Plans and billing
